KIN AND COMPASS - ACCRA 90-DAY CHECKLIST

Week 1
- Serviced apartment, not a year lease
- Local SIM and mobile money
- Yellow fever certificate copy; embassy registration
- One community: church, sport, or alumni

Month 1
- Learn the commute before signing housing
- Ask about water tanks, power backup, internet
- Immigration appointment if required

Days 60-90
- School visits if relocating with family
- Bank and tax conversations with a local adviser
- Read Invest and Study sections only after you have a routine
